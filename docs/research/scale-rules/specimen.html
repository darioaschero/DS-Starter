The parametrization branches

The controls expose independent branches instead of hiding taste inside one algorithm. The density branch is especially consequential because prose and controls consume many of the same steps.

Spacing shape

Hybrid preserves the fitted 1·2·3·4·6·8·12·16 multipliers. Geometric makes one ratio legible but lets the tail run. Linear is easy to predict and intentionally compresses large layout intervals.

Density model

Global scales every step. Curve moves small steps while holding steps 4–8. Tables permit curated shapes. None fully separates prose from controls because both consume steps 1, 2, 4, and 6.

Type voice

A 16px body anchor plus ratios from 1.125 to 1.333 ranges from quiet to declarative. Quantizing cumulatively to whole pixels reproduces the fitted base; half-pixel and exact anchored math expose different small-size and high-step behavior.

Leading and softness

Leading is a role-offset curve: smaller sizes are looser and headings are shifted tighter. Radius is a named triple—sharp, soft, or round—while full remains fixed and therefore needs no per-control tuning.

Live generator

Generated values override the page’s root custom properties. The lab controls stay fixed; the two real-system samples update in light and dark together.
